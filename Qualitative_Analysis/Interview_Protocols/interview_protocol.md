# Interview Protocol

## Opening

Thank the participant and remind them that participation is voluntary.

## Questions

1. How familiar are you with generative AI tools?
2. What tasks do you use generative AI for?
3. What benefits have you noticed?
4. What concerns or risks do you associate with these tools?
5. What guidance would help people use generative AI responsibly?

## Closing

Ask whether the participant would like to add anything else.
